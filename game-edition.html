Mission 01

Crop Yield Prediction

作物収量予測

Predicts yield for rice, wheat, maize and sugarcane from state, season, year and planted area — trained on public Indian government agricultural data, later updated to the official 1997–2022 series.

Caught a silent unit bug

Per-quantile distributions showed a 175× jump from median to Q3: some crops were logged in nuts or bales, not tonnes, with no column saying which. I scoped to four crops with self-consistent units instead of guessing conversions.

Found where the error lived

Breaking MAE down by crop: one contributed 65.15 against 0.55 for all others. Traced to physically implausible rows — removing them cut error 83%.

Honest about limits

Documented data provenance, cleaning decisions and six explicit limitations — including that a random forest can't extrapolate to future years, so it isn't sold as a forecaster.

One car broke the model

R² stalled at 0.75. Ranking the largest errors exposed a single ₹3.95 Cr supercar — the only one of its brand. Dropping brands with under 10 listings (9 of 15,407 cars) took R² from 0.72 to 0.94.

Validated properly

5-fold cross-validation on top of a held-out test set — R² between 0.90 and 0.95 on every fold, so the score isn't one lucky split.

Mission 03 ● Complete · live since Oct 25, 2026

Real-Time Fraud Detection

实时欺诈检测

A small local copy of a payment company's fraud pipeline: stream card transactions one at a time, build live per-card features, score each one in about 10 ms, explain every decision and watch for drift. Now live online. Built on IBM TabFormer, 24.4 million synthetic card transactions from 2,000 people.

Threw out the first dataset

EDA on PaySim showed senders almost never repeat and the balance columns leak the label. A per-card real-time system needs history, so I moved to IBM TabFormer.

A strong feature made it worse

Adding card-use type cut frauds in the top 1,000 alerts from 150 to 17. Online fraud fell from 66 to 0 per 10,000 after 2017 — drift, confirmed on a second group of people. The feature stays out.

Tested on people it never saw

Scored 253 new people in a later period without retraining. Per-card history features ("is this the card's usual hour?") lifted it from 142 to 177 frauds in the top 1,000 alerts, against about 1 for random guessing and 135 for XGBoost.

Real-time and live

Kafka streams each payment, Redis holds every card's history, FastAPI scores it in about 10 ms. Live features match training 100%, checked in CI. Deployed with Aiven Kafka, Redis Cloud and Streamlit: try your own purchase and watch the model react.
