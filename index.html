EXP-03 · fraud-detection-realtimerunning · updated Oct 2026

Real-Time Fraud Detection

24.4 million card transactions from 2,000 people, about 12 frauds in every 10,000 (IBM TabFormer). The hard part is not the model. It is keeping the future out of the training data.

pythonpandasscikit-learnxgboostparquet
  • Threw out the first datasetPaySim's balance columns were never updated for fraud rows, so they gave the answer away.
  • Split by time, not at randomTraining ends April 2017. The model is always judged on the future.
  • A strong feature made it worseOnline fraud fell from 66 to 0 per 10,000 after 2017. Drift, confirmed on a second group. Feature removed.
  • Held up on 253 unseen people142 frauds in the top 1,000 alerts, against 150 on its own people.
  • NextXGBoost, then experiment tracking and a FastAPI scoring service.
